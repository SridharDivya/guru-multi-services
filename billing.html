Overview

Every Bill, Itemised And Online

Every service you book — plywood order, repair visit, carpentry project or ride — generates a digital, itemised invoice in your Guru Multi Services account. Track payment status, download statements, and pay via UPI or card, all from one place.

No more paper receipts or chasing down a bill for warranty claims — your full service and payment history stays a click away.
