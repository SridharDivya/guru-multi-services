Overview

Furniture Designed Around You

Our in-house carpenters design and build modular kitchens, wardrobes, TV units and bespoke furniture, measured and fitted to your actual space — not a catalogue template. We use the same graded plywood and timber we supply, so the finish holds up for years, not seasons.

From first sketch to final polish, one designer stays with your project so nothing gets lost between site visit and site fit.

Why Choose Us

What You Get

Custom measured design

Every piece is designed to your exact room dimensions, not a standard size.

One dedicated designer

A single point of contact from design to final installation.

In-house fabrication

Cutting, edge-banding and hardware fitting done by our own workshop.

1-year guarantee

Joints, hinges and finish are covered for a full year.
