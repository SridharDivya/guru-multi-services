Overview

One Call For Every Electrical Job

From a tripping MCB and short circuits to full house rewiring, new switchboard points, fan and light installation, and inverter/stabilizer connections, our technicians handle residential and commercial electrical work of every scale — all under one booking. Every technician arrives in uniform, carries ID, and quotes the job before touching a single wire.

No job is too small: we take on single-fix visits as readily as annual maintenance contracts for apartments, shops and offices.
