Overview

Timber You Can Actually Trust

We supply marine, commercial and BWP (Boiling Water Proof) grade plywood, decorative veneers, block boards and solid timber sections. Every batch is checked for moisture content, core quality and certification before it leaves our yard, so what you receive matches exactly what you ordered — no substitutions, no surprises.

Whether you're a homeowner fitting one wardrobe or a contractor sourcing for a full site, we quote by the sheet or by the project and deliver on your schedule.

Why Choose Us

What You Get

Certified grading

Cut-to-size options and doorstep delivery within the city.

Fair per-sheet pricing whether you need one panel or a full lot.

Warranty-backed quality

BWP boards come with a manufacturer warranty against delamination.
