Overview

New Sun Direct Connections, Shifting, Repairs & Recharge

Our technicians handle new Sun Direct DTH connections from start to finish — dish alignment, set-top box setup, cabling and first-time activation, done on-site the same day you book. We also handle dish shifting when you move house, multi-TV Sun Direct setups, no-signal or weak-signal repair visits, and recharge assistance for existing Sun Direct customers.

One visit is usually enough — our technician carries the mounting hardware, cables and connectors needed to complete most Sun Direct installations without a second trip.
