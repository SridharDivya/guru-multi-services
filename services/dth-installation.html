Overview

New Connections, Shifting & Repairs — All DTH Brands

Our technicians handle new Sun Direct, Airtel Digital TV, Tata Play and Dish TV connections, along with Airtel Xstream Fiber broadband installation — dish alignment, set-top box setup, cabling and first-time activation done on-site the same day you book. We also handle dish shifting when you move house, multi-TV connections, and no-signal or weak-signal repair visits for any brand you already have installed.

One visit is usually enough — our technician carries the common mounting hardware, cables and connectors needed to complete most installations without a second trip.
