Important Information Disclaimer

Guru Multi Services is an independent auxiliary support provider. We are not affiliated with, authorized by, or officially associated with Christian Medical College (CMC) Vellore or Chittoor. We assist patients with public portal bookings, local travel arrangements, and third-party lodging guidance on an assistant-service basis.

Appointment Booking Guidance

Navigating busy portals can be daunting. We guide you through the process of setting up patient IDs and obtaining formal appointment bookings at both the Vellore and Chittoor campuses.

Wheelchair-Accessible Rooms

We connect families with verified local guest houses, apartments, and hotels that feature patient-friendly environments, elevators, step-free access, and kitchen facilities.

Airport & Rail Transfers

Book reliable pickups and transfers directly from Chennai/Bengaluru airports or Katpadi Junction station straight to your hotel or the hospital campus.

Why Coordinate Your Travel With Us?

Seeking treatment away from home is physically and emotionally exhausting. Our team works to eliminate logistical stress so you and your family can focus completely on recovery.

  • Dual Campus Coverage: Coordination support for both main town Vellore and the newer Chittoor campus.
  • Tailored Stays: Options for long-term kitchen-equipped rooms, ideal for recovery periods.
  • Transparent Costs: Zero hidden agency markups. You pay exact, direct rates for travel and lodging.
